# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First stable release. In daily use for a month; the deck-pack format and the
database schema are both versioned and migration-tested, so decks and progress
carry forward from here.

**The drill** — an arcade claw crosses the screen as your timer, over a pit of
prizes. Answer in time and it grabs one for your jar; miss and a kitchen-timer
ding sends the card back, showing the answer with its mnemonic. Correct answers
carry a spoken syllable on the kana decks.

**Perfection** — one jar slot per card, decided by your first attempt: a prize
or a grey pebble, never a percentage. A flawless session seals its jar onto a
permanent trophy shelf, where every tenth jar consolidates into a numbered
ten-jar, then a hundred-jar. Only the day's first session can seal; later runs
are labelled practice rounds from the start.

**Scheduling** — five Leitner boxes (due after 1/3/7/30 days), with timers that
tighten as cards climb. New cards arrive only on the day's first drill, and
pause entirely while box 1 is crowded, so a bad week can't bury you.

**Calibration** — before a deck's first drill, a short copy-typing warm-up
measures your motor floor; the deadline is set just above it, plus a per-deck
thinking allowance. Enough time to remember, not enough to calculate. Warm-up
trials are logged as calibration and never touch your learning state.

**Hall of Fame** — decks ranked by sealed jars, plus records (fastest answer,
largest perfect session, busiest day) and per-deck charts, on a CRT panel. A
practice-day streak with a 30-day dot strip sits in the home header.

**Decks** — hiragana and katakana (104 cards each, spoken, fully mnemonic'd)
and treble/bass piano notation with engraved clefs, attached to this release.
Author your own as a folder or zip: see the README.

Installers for Linux (deb/rpm), Windows and macOS below. The macOS app is
unsigned — right-click → Open on first launch.
